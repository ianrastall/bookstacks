# Author coverage and proposed additions

24 October 2026. Local audit of `tei/*/*.xml`, the published author profiles,
and every PNG in `public/img/authors/`. Work counts group filename identities
across languages; editions count individual XML files. These are bibliographic
coverage observations, not assertions that every transcription is complete or
free of defects. Existing corpus-quality findings remain in `corpus-audit/`.

Before this addition: **18 authors, 148 work records, 258 language editions**.
There are **58 portrait files**, of which **40 were unused**. Poe brings these
totals to **19 authors, 149 work records, 259 editions, 39 unused portraits**.
A collected volume counts once, even when it contains many separate works.

## Portrait-backed additions

busts and soft lower contours; several ancient figures are traditional artistic
representations rather than authenticated likenesses. Reuse the approved files;
do not regenerate them merely to create a catalogue entry. Filenames establish
available assets, not verified historical identity or a publication license.

the current British/Russian/ancient-philosophy concentration with short fiction,
American literature, Black writers, women writers, and modern European fiction.

## Poe edition

The requested title is **Collected Works of Mystery and Horror**. It is a
single English edition, comprising 34 shorter works and **the complete
Narrative of Arthur Gordon Pym of Nantucket**, explicitly included at Ian’s
request. The manifest `tei/poe/selection.json` records every selection,
source edition, illustration subject and source checksum. The machine-readable
`import-report.json` records source block counts and normalized text hashes.

The selected shorter works cover detection (*Rue Morgue*, *Marie Roget*,
*The Purloined Letter*, *The Gold-Bug*, *Thou Art the Man*), psychological and
burial horror, Gothic/supernatural tales, sea terror, and a few closely related
uncanny tales. *Eleonora*, *Mesmeric Revelation*, *The Conversation of Eiros and
Charmion*, *Silence*, and *The Man of the Crowd* are deliberate broader-edge
choices: supernatural romance, death/mesmerism, apocalypse, uncanny fable, and
urban enigma respectively. *The System of Doctor Tarr and Professor Fether*
retains black comedy within its asylum-horror setting.

This is an editorial selection, not a claim to every possible horror-adjacent
Poe text. Standalone poems, essays, biography, travel/scientific hoaxes and
predominantly comic sketches are excluded. Examples: *The Raven*, *The Bells*,
*The Philosophy of Composition*, *Maelzel’s Chess-Player*, *Hans Pfaall*,
*The Balloon-Hoax*, *Some Words with a Mummy*, *King Pest*, *Loss of Breath*,
*Never Bet the Devil Your Head*, *The Angel of the Odd*. Atmospheric landscape
pieces *The Island of the Fay*, *The Domain of Arnheim* and *Landor’s Cottage*
are also excluded. Verse and epigraphs embedded in selected stories remain.

The shorter works use Project Gutenberg’s Raven Edition, volumes
[I / 2147](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2147),
[II / 2148](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2148),
[III / 2149](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2149),
[IV / 2150](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2150),
[V / 2151](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2151). Pym uses the
[New York 1838 edition / 51060](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/51060),
transcribed by Ron Swanson. This source retains the original cave diagrams and
hieroglyphic figures; the older Raven transcription explicitly omits them.
Electronic texts credit David Widger, with Carlo Traverso also credited in I.
Each chosen source edition’s spelling and editorial apparatus are retained.
Notes gathered at the end of Raven volume II are carried into the relevant
selected story. *Pym* retains its preface, all 25 chapters, original concluding
note, seven source-image files (four diagram plates and three glyph images),
and the transcriber’s explanation of the original duplicate chapter numbering.
The source diagrams and glyphs remain with the passages they explain; they
are distinct from the new decorative engravings in the sidebar.

The illustration plan is **36 original plates**: one per shorter work and two
for *Pym*. These are new AI-generated interpretations in nineteenth-century
steel-engraving style, not reproductions attributed to historical engravers.
All four edges fade into the page; the web treatment blends with both light
and dark reading surfaces. Each page displays its plate beneath the right-hand
navigation, outside the prose. Pym uses the voyage image through chapter 22
and the Antarctic image for chapters 23–25 and its concluding note. On phones,
the plate follows the stacked navigation. Prompts and asset provenance are stored beside
the TEI. Approved author portraits are unchanged.

`python tei/build_poe.py` is an explicit, checksum-guarded offline import. It
is not part of `npm run build`, site deployment, or the PDF pipeline. No
existing TEI editions or PDF publications are regenerated by this addition.
