# bookstacks: project brief

Working brief · 21 October 2026

## Purpose and priority

Publish readable, semantically encoded literary editions and their source/publication downloads.

Priority: important. Ordering within the five important projects remains open.

## Product track

Astro/TypeScript reading site plus editorial/publication pipelines.

Acquisition, semantic encoding and publications are subprojects; upstream TEI standards and literary corpora are references. Earlier site versions are sources to compare, not new products.

## Preserve

Canonical TEI meaning, editorial conventions, language/edition identities, stable reading links, notes, source credits, approved artwork and XML/PDF publication boundaries.

## First planning outcome

Separate library discovery, edition/contents and the reading experience. Plan a reader concept independently of the general website/workbench concept.

## Evidence of readiness for a technical scaffold

A canonical-source/edition/publication map and checkout comparison are recorded; reading size, navigation, language behavior and note access are specified before a skeleton.
