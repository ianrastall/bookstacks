# English editions of the Chinese works

Source review: 24 October 2026. Ian requested complete, freely reusable English
translations. Translation rights and completeness are checked independently of
the public-domain Chinese original. No abridgement is published as a complete
edition, and a downloadable file alone does not establish reuse permission.

| Work | Result | Source evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tao Te Ching | Added James Legge’s complete English translation, both parts and all 81 chapters. | [Gutenberg 216](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/216) identifies Legge, English, public-domain status in the USA, and transcription credits. `tei/build_legge_tao.py` records the source fingerprint and preserves every literary block and verse line. |
| Call to Arms | Awaiting a complete translation with clear reuse terms. | The [Wikisource community translation](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Translation:Call_to_Arms_(Lu_Xun)) has free-license notices but explicitly says it is incomplete (reviewed revision 10817621). The [translation index](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Call_to_Arms_(Lu_Xun)) also lists the modern Yang translation; its listing does not grant republication rights. |
| Journey to the West | Awaiting a complete translation with clear reuse terms. | [Columbia’s teaching bibliography](https://columbia.edu/cu/weai/exeas/resources/teaching-journeytowest-worldlit.html) distinguishes complete Yu/Jenner translations from abridgements. [Mouseia](https://mouseia.org/texts/journey-to-the-west) offers complete machine-assisted versions, but its [terms](https://mouseia.org/terms) do not specify a clear downstream reuse license: they grant hosting/distribution rights to Mouseia and allow others to read and link. This does not establish permission to republish those translations in Bookstacks. |

To finish the remaining editions, supply a complete translation with a suitable
license or permission covering republication. Preserve its translator credits,
notes, front matter and independent chapter hierarchy in a new `_eng.xml` file
under the existing author/work identity. No English placeholders or partial
editions have been added for the two unresolved works.
